### 👋 About us

Auxilium Global Softech Pvt. Ltd. makes business software. Our product, **Sares**, runs a company's inventory, accounts, customers and people in one system. When a deal closes in CRM, inventory, invoicing and payroll already know about it, so nobody re-types data between tools or reconciles spreadsheets at month end.

### 🧩 Sares at a glance

| | Module | What it covers |
|:-:|---|---|
| 📦 | **Inventory** | Items, parties, purchase and sales with approvals, stock transfers, returns, barcodes, forecasting and stock registers |
| 💰 | **Accounts** | Invoices, receipts, payments, vouchers, tax management, trial balance, ledgers and outstanding |
| 🤝 | **CRM** | Leads, a drag-and-drop deal pipeline, campaigns, calls, meetings and quotations |
| 👥 | **HRM** | Employees, shifts, attendance, leave, payroll, payslips and hiring |
| ⚙️ | **Admin** | Users, roles, role-based access and company settings |

### ✨ What makes Sares different

- 🌍 **Tax that follows the location.** GST in India with CGST, SGST, IGST, HSN and TDS/TCS. VAT in the Gulf, the UK and the EU, and sales tax in the US. Packs cover 19 countries.
- 💱 **Live currency conversion.** Every entry keeps its own currency and is shown in yours at the current market rate.
- 🌐 **14 languages**, from Arabic to Turkish.
- 🌓 **Light and dark mode**, on every screen from phones to 4K monitors.
- ⌨️ **Keyboard first.** One search finds any page or record.
